# Sherlock integration

HuntWick uses the MIT-licensed [`sherlock-project` 0.16.2 release](https://github.com/sherlock-project/sherlock/tree/a38ba54fda799cd786a2ab67a50143e1a63169e6). It calls `sherlock_project.sherlock.sherlock()` and `SitesInformation` from this package. The adapter supplies its own bounded HTTP transport and checks response evidence before reporting a match. The [upstream usage documentation](https://sherlockproject.xyz/usage) describes the standalone Sherlock CLI.

`data/sites.json` is the verbatim [upstream site manifest](https://github.com/sherlock-project/sherlock/blob/4e2a4f6b66433849abe46cae76ed557c6c8313c8/sherlock_project/resources/data.json), last changed May 4, 2026. Its contents match the snapshot in the 0.16.2 release. `data/exclusions.txt` comes from the [upstream false-positive exclusion list](https://github.com/sherlock-project/sherlock/blob/7ce4f611a1a82b204cd6b20b65361b55c98f67b3/false_positive_exclusions.txt), updated November 22, 2026. HuntWick bundled both on November 22, 2026. The app reports the upstream site-manifest change date and both revisions. See [Sherlock-MIT.txt](Sherlock-MIT.txt) for the copyright and license.

The catalog applies Sherlock's standard adult-site exclusions, excludes unsupported or insecure probes and retains HTTPS GET public-profile rules. HuntWick supplies categories and the Quick selection. Database updates ship with app releases. No manifest download occurs at launch. To refresh a snapshot, review the upstream definitions and exclusions, replace these files, update their digests, revision and date in `catalog.py`, regenerate `public/catalog.json`, then run the engine suite and packaged smoke check.
