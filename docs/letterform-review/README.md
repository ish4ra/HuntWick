# HuntWick wordmark review

Each sheet pairs the proposed letter with the original lowercase i from the HuntWick vector wordmark. The top half shows the dark palette and the lower half shows the light palette. View the PNG at 100% to see the two 220 px sidebar headers at their intended size. Each SVG contains the reusable glyph in the group named `letter`.

| Study | How the i works | Preview | Vector |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Air | A small open ring sits above a gently inclined stem. The gap carries the rhythm of a dotted i at sidebar size. | [PNG](air.png) | [SVG](air.svg) |
| Tangent | A narrow joint at the lower-right edge of the lens opens into a continuous handle. Its foot lands on the same baseline as the c. | [PNG](tangent.png) | [SVG](tangent.svg) |
| Cut | A short diagonal leaves the lens and meets a matching cut at the head of an upright stem. The gap separates dot and stem, while the diagonal gives the dot its search silhouette. | [PNG](cut.png) | [SVG](cut.svg) |

The owner selected **Cut** for RC.3. Desktop, web and the README hero use its approved outlines in both theme variants. These sheets preserve the original review.
